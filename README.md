# 📖 About

T-Strike is an action-packed typing game built for developers, students, gamers, and anyone looking to improve their typing skills in a fun and engaging way.

Instead of traditional typing lessons, players battle waves of enemies by typing the words displayed on screen before they reach the player.

Every correct keystroke contributes to defeating enemies, making typing practice exciting and rewarding.

---

# 🎮 Gameplay

Your mission is simple.

- Type the displayed words as quickly as possible.
- Destroy enemies before they reach you.
- Earn points for every successful strike.
- Survive increasingly difficult waves.
- Improve both typing speed and accuracy.

The faster and more accurately you type, the higher your score.
